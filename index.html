Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

What We Collect

When you log in with Instagram, we collect your Instagram username and access token. If you use the DM tracking feature, we store metadata about messages sent to store accounts (recipient, timestamp, and status).

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to help you track outreach to independent grocery stores. We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than powering the features you interact with.

Storage

Data is stored securely in Supabase (a hosted PostgreSQL service). Access is restricted to authenticated users viewing their own data.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Revoking the app's access in your Instagram settings will also stop any further data collection.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
